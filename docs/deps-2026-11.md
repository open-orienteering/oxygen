# November 2026 dependency sweep, part 1 (minor / patch)

The first of a stack of dependency PRs planned on 2026-11-20 after v0.9.0. This one raises every direct dependency to its newest minor or patch release in its current major, refreshes the lockfile and the advisory overrides, and adds a 7-day release-age hold. The majors each get their own PR (AGENTS.md §14): Node 24, Playwright 1.65, Vitest 5, i18next 27, then vite-plugin-pwa 2 / dotenv 18 / GitHub Actions. Previous sweep: [`deps-2026-10.md`](deps-2026-10.md).

## 7-day release-age hold

`pnpm-workspace.yaml` now sets `minimumReleaseAge: 10080` (minutes). pnpm will not resolve any version, direct or transitive, published less than 7 days ago. A broken or compromised publish usually gets pulled within days, so the hold keeps it out of the lockfile. A security fix that can't wait is added to `minimumReleaseAgeExclude` with a comment.

## Overrides

All overrides were kept. Dropping them all as a test showed that `effect`, `find-my-way` and `mysql2` fall back below their advisory floors without them. Two floors moved up for new advisories:

- `fast-uri@<3.1.9` → `^3.1.9` and `fast-uri@>=4.0.0 <4.2.3` → `^4.2.3` (GHSA-333x-w4r6-6crp and related).
- `serialize-javascript` → `^7.1.3`.

`pnpm run audit:prod --audit-level=low`: no advisories in the 434-package production tree (before: 6 low, 8 moderate). The full `pnpm audit` still reports three moderate dev-only advisories. One is `glob` 11 under `workbox-build`, which needs glob 14, a cross-major jump inside the service worker build. The other two are `minimatch` 10 under `@typescript-eslint/typescript-estree`, which needs 11. Both are build-time only and left for upstream.
