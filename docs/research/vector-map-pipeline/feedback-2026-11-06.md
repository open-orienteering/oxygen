# User feedback after slice E (2026-11-06) and the rows P1–P3, F0

Recorded by the coordinator. The user tried the v0.6.0 candidate (`52b13a0`:
the flip, E1–E4c, E6) and said it is good; these points are **not** blocking
slice E ("getting E fully out first") and become rows after it. Nothing here
is planned in detail yet: each row's first step is `slice-planner` (Fable).

## The feedback

1. Placing a new control still draws a thin blue circle beside the purple
   one. Only the purple is wanted. (Feedback 2026-11-04 item 3 / row D12
   removed the selection ring while a tool is armed; a blue circle is still
   there.)
2. Layout mode, toner saver: text still shows through outside the saver, and
   the forbidden (out-of-bounds) areas do too.
3. The status bar (the footer of the course editor) takes space on small
   laptop screens. Move it, perhaps all of it, into a separate dialog.
4. Corridors: are they in the roadmap?
5. Map simplification, perhaps during import: the filter region's symbol
   list has too many of the same thing, odd colours and frames. Flattening
   would be easier to use. A symbol picker by clicking on the map as well.
