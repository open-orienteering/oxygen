## Defaults and decisions (for user review unless answered)

- F13:
  - Bends are path vertices (offsets) with optional fields, so there is no
    migration.
  - The derivation flattens at 5 µm, and every consumer reads the flattened
    polyline. Legs stay polylines in the PDF; course objects keep cubics.
  - A crossing point joins a curved leg as a corner on the nearest curved
    segment.
  - Lengths follow the flattened curve.
  - Corridor runs are simplified at 20 µm only if a curved leg costs more
    than 3× a straight one.
  - Purple Pen export follows the user's answer to Q3 (default: flattened at
    0.1 mm).
- F14:
  - `legLine()` is 705 unless the leg has a corridor; 707 always draws;
    `none` never does.
  - The guide is always on wherever legs are clickable, and never in All
    variations, Layout, Field or print.
  - `G` still writes gaps on a corridor leg.
- F15:
  - The rule is the user's answer to Q4. Default: numbers when every visited
    site has exactly one sequence number across all variants; loops and
    unequal forks keep codes.
  - It applies on screen and on the `?allVariations=` page alike.
  - Labels travel as `viewOptions.labels`.
- F16:
  - The gestures are the user's answer to Q2. Default: the area tools'
    gestures — a new bend is a corner, Alt-drag pulls symmetric handles,
    grips drag one handle, Alt-click retracts.
  - Smooth / Corner buttons on the bend row cover tablets.
  - Touch bend drags stay Pending.
- F17:
  - Roots and variants share one expanded row and the same `?course=` param.
  - `CourseInlineDetail` gains `readOnly`.
  - No new button in the row.

## Answers (user 2026-11-08)

1. Handles on bends only.
2. **Smooth by default**: "I think I want to make areas and such default to
   smoothing as well, with alt being the modifier to make them sharp."
   - A new bend is smooth (handles from its neighbours), and Alt makes it
     sharp (F16).
   - The same inversion of D15 applies to every vertex tool, in Course
     mode's areas and lines and in Layout mode's paths. That is the new
     row **F18**, which runs before F16 so the bends use the shared
     convention.
   - Stored corners (imports, earlier drawings) stay corners.
   - Touch (F18's question, answered the same day): with no Alt on touch,
     taps follow the default and place smooth vertices (option a, not b:
     taps kept as corners). Corners on touch wait for a vertex action bar
     with Smooth / Corner buttons.
3. Purple Pen: flatten at 0.1 mm.
4. All variations: numbers when every site has one number across all
   variants, otherwise codes. The block follows where it is cheap
   (planner's default).

### F19 plan (Fable planner, condensed)

- Everything stays in `shared/src/course/{corridor,layer,symbols}.ts`. Screen
  and print both read `courseLayer`, and `derive.ts` / `types.ts` stay
  untouched, so F19 does not collide with F15.
- **Mouths.** Each site where a corridor leg (edged or not) starts or ends
  adds its symbol's extent to the corridor's inside union, and that extent
  plus the largest `whiteMm` to the band. The extent is the control disc at
  the centre-line radius R, the finish's outer ring, or the turned 701
  triangle. So the map shows inside the symbol, the band wraps it, and the
  edge pieces end where the straight sides meet the symbol.
- **Openings** (controls only). An edged corridor leg with half width h < R
  opens the circle over `θ ± asin(h/R)`, where θ is the leg's end bearing,
  if both junction points (offset ±h, `√(R² − h²)` along the leg) lie on
  the inside's boundary. Openings are drawn, not cuts: they are not stored,
  not in `DerivedSite.cuts`, not under `autoCuts`, and `G` never bakes them
  into `circleGaps`.
- The edge is a derived round-capped 705 (`<705>/edge`), so the join leaves
  no notch.
- A corridor passing over a circle it does not end at leaves that circle
  whole.
- The `course-corridor` scene is re-shaped (one narrow run, one wide leg),
  and its goldens, the PDF case and the F3 corridor E2E are extended.

### F19 answers (user 2026-11-08)

1. Corridor as wide as or wider than the circle (h ≥ R): **the circle sits
   inside the corridor**. Today's geometry is kept: the round end encloses
   the whole circle, and nothing opens.
2. Start and finish: **the edges stop at them**. 701 and 706 are mouths,
   the edges end on the triangle or outer ring, the map shows inside, and
   the band wraps them. Neither symbol is ever cut (§3.3 kept).
3. `edge: false`: **the map shows and the circle stays whole**. The mouth
   applies and nothing opens.

### F20 plan (Fable planner, measured)

- **Bench.** Synthetic 25-control course with one F18-style smooth bend per
  leg, at 1:15 000. Scripts:
  `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-marcus-IdeaProjects-orientering-oxygen/8107d8bd-4d39-4b35-9ed7-6ec9e1370a4a/scratchpad/f20-*.ts`.
- **Points.** The smooth bends take the course from 52 points to 1100, and
  `deriveCourse` from 69 ms to 400 ms. `placeControlLabels` accounts for
  376 ms of that, since it is O(labels × candidates × all segments).
  Corridors add 126–230 ms of `corridorGeometry`, and `polylineLegGaps`
  takes 22–40 ms at 3000 features. Flattening itself costs 0.1 ms, so the
  5 µm tolerance is not the lever, and it stays.
- **No coalescing.** `MapViewer` posts a course request per pointer move,
  and the worker runs every stale one to completion (`worker.ts:349`)
  before `engine.ts:208` drops its reply. One second of drag queues about
  24 s of worker time.
- **F20 fixes:**
  1. `MapGlEngine.setCourse` keeps one request in flight, latest wins.
  2. `placeControlLabels` tests only the segments within reach of a
     circle's candidate boxes. The output is bit-identical: a prototype
     gave 0 mismatches on 150 seeded courses, and the placer dropped from
     376–1490 ms to 30–105 ms.
  - Tests: an engine test, a placer reference fixture recorded before the
    change, and loose budget tests.
  - Docs: `docs/bugfix-course-drag-redraw.md`.
  - No user questions: no recorded decision changes.
- **F20b** (after F19, same files): a per-run ring cache in `corridor.ts`,
  adaptive `edgesOf` sampling, a `polylineLegGaps` feature prefilter by the
  leg's bounding box, and optionally `fastFilter` on drags over corridor
  courses.
