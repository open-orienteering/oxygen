# User feedback on corridors (2026-11-09) and the rows F21–F22

Recorded by the coordinator. The user tried the branch after F19, F20 and
F20b had merged (`3f4670b7`). A Fable `slice-planner` wrote both plans,
and the user answered the open points (below). Each row's session still
runs `slice-planner` first and may reopen a default, with a reason.

## The feedback

1. "If I move the endpoint of a corridor, it redraws the map while moving.
   But if I move a midpoint somewhere, it doesn't. And it needs to, if not
   it's not possible to correctly place the corridor in a usable manner."
2. "I think the white area at the start/end of a corridor is a bit too
   big. The way coursecraft does it is that it doesn't extend further than
   the middle of the control circle. I think I would rather round it off
   towards the center of the circle, or towards the end of the corridor
   even, not sure what looks best. But ofc if the control is inside of a
   corridor that continues on both sides, the whiteout shouldn't get
   thinner."
3. Fix the other flaky test (F15 "All variations", the follow-up of
   PR #158). It runs as its own bug-fix session, not a row.

## Root causes

- **Item 1.** A control drag sets `editorDragPos`, which feeds the course
  request in `MapViewer.tsx` (`courseRequest` memo), so the layer
  re-derives on every move (coalesced by F20). A bend or bend-grip drag
  (`bindings.ts`, gestures `bend` / `bend-grip`) redraws only the UI
  layer's blue dashed outline and the handles. The layer gets the new
  `bends` only on release (`onBendsChange` → `writeLeg`). This is what F16
  recorded on purpose ("no layer request per move"), and the feedback
  reverses it.
- **Item 2.** `course-layer.md` §10.2: the band is the run's buffer at
  `h + whiteMm`, with a round cap about the end site, plus the mouth's
  symbol grown by `whiteMm` (F19), less the inside. So the white wraps the
  whole end circle, behind it as well.

## Answers (user, 2026-11-09)

1. **End shape: option 3, "flat, corners rounded".** At a corridor's
   end, the band stops on the line through the circle's centre, across
   the leg, and its outer corners are rounded by `whiteMm`. The near half
   of the circle is still wrapped by `whiteMm`. The four candidates were
   shown as sketches:
   1. flat at the centre;
   2. a round end with its tip at the centre;
   3. flat with rounded corners;
   4. a round end with its tip at the corridor's end.

   In the planner's parametrisation this is `TERMINAL_END = { setback: 0,
   cap: "flat", corner: whiteMm }` with the mouth's grown symbol `"half"`.
2. **The start keeps today's geometry.** The run ends flat at the
   triangle and the band frames the whole triangle. The finish is a
   terminal end like a control, with R = 706's outer ring.
3. **A wide corridor (h ≥ R) keeps its round edge.** Only the white
   changes: the inside, and so the edge line round the end circle, are
   untouched (F19 answer 1 stands). The edge's half-circle behind the
   circle now lies on the map.
4. **"Never thinner":** an end is *terminal* only where exactly one
   corridor-leg end lies at that site, counted over every course. These
   all keep today's join:
   - a control inside a run;
   - a change of width or band;
   - two courses' corridors meeting;
   - a loop or a reversal.

   A corridor that only passes over a terminal site keeps its band
   whole.
5. Start F21 and F22 in parallel. F22 merges first, and F21 then appends
   its E2E test.

## F21 plan: live course redraw during a bend or grip drag

**Scope.** While a bend handle or bend grip of the selected leg is dragged
in `V`, the gesture's live `bends` reach the course request on every move.
The layer then re-derives the whole course as the write would leave it,
coalesced by F20 (one request in flight):
- the purple line or 707 pieces, and their cuts;
- the numbers;
- the corridor's inside, band, edge and openings, and every drawn
  course's union.

The release writes once, as today (`onBendsChange` → `writeLeg`). These
clear the draft:
- a click;
- an Alt-click toggle;
- a cancelled drag (`onPointerUp(null)`, a mouseleave);
- Escape;
- a leg change;
- undo or redo (`moveEpoch`);
- a failed write.

After a release the draft is held until `course.list` reports the written
bends, the same way `pendingMoves` does for controls, so the band doesn't
jump back between the write and the refetch. This applies to every leg of
the selected course, corridor or not, in every view where a leg is
selectable.

**Not in scope:**
- touch bend drags (still Pending; the draft doesn't depend on the pointer
  type, so touch gets it when it lands);
- a mouse loupe;
- live inspector metres;
- any change to the corridor's cost.

**Steps.**
1. Pure functions `withLegDraft(courses, draft)` and `bendsEqual`.
   - `withLegDraft` goes in `lib/editor/variation-view.ts`, next to
     `withCourseOverride` and applied after it, with
     `interface LegDraft { course; key: "<fromId>><toId>"; bends }`. It
     patches only that leg's `bends`; `style`, `gaps`, `corridor` and
     `crossing` stay.
   - `bendsEqual` goes in `lib/editor/bends.ts`.
   - Tests first: `variation-view.test.ts`, `bends.test.ts`, and one
     `course-layer-request.test.ts` case.
2. The binding reports the draft.
   - `LegBends.onBendsDraft?(bends | null)` is called from `onPointerDrag`
     for `bend` (after `moveBend`, snapped) and `bend-grip` (after
     `gripDragged`).
   - On release, it calls `onBendsDraft(null)` only on the cancel and
     no-leg paths and for a click without movement. A committing release
     calls `onBendsChange` only.
   - Tests in `bindings.test.ts` "V on a selected leg":
     - a drag reports past 3 px, never before;
     - a release writes once;
     - a cancelled drag clears the draft and doesn't write;
     - a click gives no draft;
     - a grip drag reports its mirrored pair, with Alt live;
     - an Alt-click toggles with no draft.
3. Page state and the viewer prop.
   - `EditorPage.tsx` keeps a `legDraft` state with a `pending` flag.
     `onBendsChange` turns the draft pending, and `writeLeg` returns its
     promise, so a failure clears the draft.
   - Effects clear the draft when `course.list`'s leg `bendsEqual` the
     pending one, when the selected leg changes, and on `moveEpoch`.
   - `MapViewer.tsx` gets `MapViewerEditorProps.legDraft` and applies
     `withLegDraft(withCourseOverride(…), legDraft)` in the same memo.
4. E2E: `e2e/course-editor.spec.ts`, F3 describe. **Append** the new test
   at the end of the describe, plus a `dragHold` helper in
   `e2e/helpers/editor.ts`.
   - The test: a two-control course with the default corridor, then *Add
     bend here*. Hold the mouse on the handle and move it perpendicular
     by more than h + whiteMm.
   - While the button is still down:
     - `expect.poll(inkAt(bandAtNewApex, "map")).toBe(0)`;
     - the map shows beside the chord midpoint;
     - no `course.setStructure` call has gone out yet.
   - The release makes one write, and the probes still hold afterwards.
     Ctrl+Z restores the chord band.
   - Also check the purple apex on a plain leg before release.
5. Docs:
   - `course-editor.md` §6.7 ("Drag a bend", "Drag a grip", "Writes") and
     its Tests table;
   - `course-layer.md` §5 (the `leg` row: "or the editor's leg draft
     while a bend or grip is dragged, merged after the course override")
     and the §10.2 Pending "Per-request cost";
   - `docs/features.md` (the leg sentence);
   - the decision-log row (below) and the SLICES row.

**Decided in this plan.**
- The draft is a separate `legDraft`, not a widened `courseOverride`. The
  override also replaces the controls, and a drag must not move the drawn
  start on a legacy course.
- It has a pending phase after release, like `pendingMoves`.
- `onBendsDraft` is optional, so existing callers are unchanged.
- There's no `fastFilter` or approximate corridor while dragging:
  identity-only corridor levers are the user's rule (F20b).
- The F20b key spans every course's corridor-leg points. So a bend drag on
  a corridor leg recomputes all the corridors per request, and a drag on a
  non-corridor leg reuses the kept result.

**Decision-log row (to adapt):**
- *Decision:* F21: the layer follows a bend or grip drag live. This
  deviates from F16 §6.7 ("no layer request per move") and from the
  `course-layer.md` Pending "the bend drag previews on the UI layer".
- *Change:* the selected leg's live bends go into the course request on
  every move (`legDraft`, merged after the course override), coalesced by
  F20. There's one write on release, and the draft is kept until
  `course.list` carries the written bends. Touch stays Pending.
- *Why:* user feedback 2026-11-09 item 1.

## F22 plan: the band at a corridor's ends

**Scope.** At a **terminal end**, the white band changes from today's
round wrap to option 3. A terminal end is a corridor leg's end at a site
where no other corridor leg (any course, any width, any band) starts or
ends. Today's round wrap is at `h + whiteMm` about the site, plus the
mouth's symbol grown by `whiteMm` all round. Option 3 is the band cut
flat on the line through the site's centre, perpendicular to the end
segment's bearing there. Its outer corners are rounded with radius
`whiteMm`, and the mouth's grown symbol is clipped to its near half (the
side the leg comes from).

**Only the outer union changes.** These stay bit-identical to the F20b
reference, proven by per-component hashes:
- the inside;
- so also the edges, which are the inside's boundary;
- the openings.

Non-terminal ends keep today's join (answer 4). The start keeps today's
end (answer 2), and the finish is a terminal end with R = the 706 outer
ring. `edge: false` legs get the same end, since the band doesn't depend
on the edge. A terminal end without a mouth (no `mouths`, or an undrawn
site) cuts at the leg's end point (R = 0).

**Steps.**
1. Prep: per-component `sha256` (inside, band, edges, openings) in
   `__fixtures__/corridor-reference.json`, recorded on today's geometry.
   The combined digest stays byte-equal, which proves the prep moved
   nothing. Keep the pre-F22 inside, edges and openings hashes, so their
   identity is asserted by a test.
2. Parametrise the cap in `corridor.ts`.
   - `type EndCap = "round" | "flat" | { setback; cap; corner? }`.
   - `runRing(pts, h, capStart, capEnd)` replaces the two booleans
     (today's 701 flat end = `"flat"`).
   - `Run` gains outer caps that only the outer buffer uses.
   - Tests first: the cap points.
3. The terminal rule and the half mouth.
   - `terminalEnds(ends)` returns the site ids with exactly one end over
     all courses.
   - Those runs get the outer cap `TERMINAL_END`, except at a 701, which
     stays as today.
   - A terminal mouth's grown ring is clipped to the half-plane
     `(p − c)·u ≤ 0`.
   - `corridorGeometry(courses, mouths, options?: { terminalEnd })`, with
     the option in the kept-result key. The production value is the
     exported constant `TERMINAL_END`.
4. Re-record and prove.
   - Re-record with `UPDATE_CORRIDOR_REFERENCE=1`, `UPDATE_STREAMS=1`
     (`course-corridor`) and `golden:render`.
   - A locality property test: band membership differs only within
     `max(h + w, R + w) + 20 µm` of a terminal site.
   - The PR records the pixel diff: every changed golden pixel lies near
     site 33.
5. E2E: in the F3 corridor test (edited **inside** the existing test), the
   map shows behind the end control, and the band probes along the middle
   are still white.
6. Docs:
   - `course-layer.md` §10.2: a new "Ends" bullet; "Mouths" amended
     (the band wraps the near half up to the centre line at a corridor's
     end, and all round where corridors continue); "The last result is
     kept" (the option is in the key); Tests; Pending;
   - `map-rendering.md` §6.3 (the `course-corridor` row);
   - `course-editor.md` §6.7 (the corridor paragraph);
   - `docs/features.md`;
   - the decision-log rows (below) and the SLICES row.

**Tests:**
- `corridor.test.ts` describe "terminal ends (F22)":
  - a lone leg with h < R;
  - h ≥ R: the inside's round end and the edge's closed ring stay, and
    there's no band beyond the centre line;
  - a run through a control: the middle is kept and both outer ends are
    cut;
  - a width change;
  - two courses meeting;
  - a reversal A→B→A;
  - a passing corridor over a terminal site;
  - a finish;
  - a start: unchanged;
  - `expectSimple` and the corner arcs of radius w;
  - two options in a row give two results.
- `corridor-reference.test.ts`: the identity of the inside, edges and
  openings, plus locality.
- `scenes.test.ts`, the goldens and the PDF tier.
- `write-page.test.ts` and `hit.test.ts` stay green.
- The F3 corridor E2E.

**Decision-log rows (to adapt):**
- F22, the band stops at a corridor's end.
  - *Deviates from:* F19 ("that shape grown by the largest `whiteMm` …
    the band wraps it") and from F13's round cap at `h + whiteMm` at a
    run end.
  - *Change:* at a terminal end (one corridor-leg end at the site over
    every course), the outer ring ends flat on the centre line with its
    corners rounded by `whiteMm`, and the mouth's grown symbol is halved.
    Non-terminal ends keep the F19 join. The inside, edges and openings
    are bit-identical. The finish works like a control, and the start
    stays as in F19.
  - *Re-recorded:* `corridor-reference.json` (n of 66 cases' band
    changed), and the `course-corridor` stream and goldens (k pixels
    within r px of site 33).
  - *Why:* user feedback 2026-11-09 item 2, answers 1–4.
- F22, `corridorGeometry` takes an options argument: `{ terminalEnd }` is
  in the kept-result key; the layer passes none.
